  • parser. The shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so day and month swap. This is the bug #4471 names.
  • hint. The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. Its tenants write the day first, so people type month first because the hint tells them to, and the form reads the date day first. The fix is the hint in booking-v3, not the parser.
  • shift. A few reports describe a date that moves by one day, not a swap: “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” A month-first parser cannot do that. The desk already read these as one-day moves (desk-notes.json); they point at time-zone handling.

Why they were merged

Decision 0001 merges reports on subject alone, and all three problems arrive with the same subjects (“Wrong day on the invite”, “Date is wrong”). Intake’s tag does not separate them either: it guesses from the reporter’s browser, not from the tenant and the form they use.

How the count was made

  • One id, one report: the ticket export repeats reports across its pages.
  • The cause comes from the tenant and the channel (causes.ts), as on the #4471 page. Reports the desk read as a one-day move count as shift, not under the tenant’s cause.
  • Every body was read: apart from the one-day moves, each describes a day and month that swapped.
